Research

I'm doing undergraduate research with Dr. Chris Marriott at UW Tacoma on a heterogeneous hexagonal cellular automaton. That's a grid of hexagonal cells that change over time based on simple rules.

Different phases show up in the grid as it runs. I'm investigating whether those phases are tied to particular genome structures and transition rules. It's my first research project, so a lot of it is writing code to run experiments and learning the concepts as I go.
