Artifact 4

For Someone Else: Genome Phase Transition

A research tool built for Dr. Chris Marriott at UW Tacoma. It extends his hexagonal cellular automaton with genome and lineage coloring, ancestry tracking, and explorers for studying whether phases correspond to particular genomes and transition rules.

View repository ↗Live simulation ↗

Reflection: I started by cloning his hex repo and doing a deep analysis of it. After figuring out what our goals were, i added a lineage and genome tracker tht identified each hex and its family to see how it evovled over time. This also allowed me to see any evolutionary patterns and adaptations.

Artifact 5

The Moonshot: GameTime

A team project: a vertical, swipe-to-discover feed of live gaming streams, like a For You page where every video is live. One Expo codebase for iOS, Android and web, backed by Supabase. My contribution brings The Biosphere Realm into it as a candidate original game that streamers can play and stream.

View repository ↗Live app ↗My pull request ↗

Reflection: I started by analyzing my teammate's repo to understand the product plan, the database, and what was still undecided before writing any code. Then I brought my own game, The Biosphere Realm, into the project as a candidate original game through a pull request that passed all of the team's checks. I learned how to join an existing codebase, follow its rules for branches, pull requests and handover notes, and fit my work into someone else's plan instead of my own.
